# ADR: Make capability accounting primary and deliver reviewed Godx behavior

## Status
accepted  (2026-09-10)

## Context

Decide scope, composition authority, priority and agent execution for the systemic “không miss” complaint. The fixed weights are coverage 25, correctness/international integrity 25, codebase fit 20, cost/load 15 and reversibility 15. Seven-pass admission, no duplication, house vocabulary, international behavior, tokens and exclusive file ownership are binding; C explicitly challenges admission. (`00-Topic.md:9-25,60-79,100-104,119-127`.) Debate-relative paths in this ADR resolve from this directory; `src/`, `docs/`, `scripts/`, `.claude/` and `CLAUDE.md` resolve from the repository root.

The entire topic, bus, five research dossiers and R2 report were read. The 127 entries and six agents are the topic's dated baseline, not a current capacity measurement (`00-Topic.md:28-51`). Current source exports Tree at `src/components/data-display/tree.tsx:567-570`, despite the older absence statement at `docs/roadmap/tree-components.md:3-15`. Source presence does not establish acceptance. The catalog guard checks reciprocal names, explicitly not advice (`scripts/check-catalog-contradictions.mjs:9-16,34-46`). Cascader's documented search-only acceptance missed parent drilling, deep hover and nested controls; this is historical evidence, not a reproduced current defect (`.claude/skills/godxjp-ui-component/SKILL.md:143-155`).

**Explicit assumptions:** (1) “không miss” means accountable discovery and disposition, not compulsory shipment; this interpretation follows the five statuses and exclusions (`docs/roadmap/antd-parity.md:9-39`). (2) The reference scope will be pinned, and existing specifications remain commitments subject to current-source reconciliation. (3) Reviewer availability, Platform fixtures, compliant ownership handoffs and available September 10–11 capacity are **[unsupported] operating assumptions**, not established facts. (4) All allocations below are proposed spending caps, not measured completion estimates. (5) The Topbar recurrence and Cascader mode-break challenges are counterfactual probes; no current regression or runtime pass is asserted. (6) Future demand, avoided maintenance cost and comparative delivery speed are unmeasured (`02-Research/ADV-A.md:115,128-134`; `02-Research/ADV-B.md:116-118`; `02-Research/ADV-D.md:131-133`).

**Bias controls applied:** R0 blindness concerns the advocates; this R3 review requires all files, and does not retroactively certify their isolation. I counted distinct mechanisms once, gave repetition and dossier length no credit, and did not discount D merely for being longest. I evaluated eligibility and the five criteria before selecting the primary. A receives zero credit for being first-listed, already running, or avoiding sunk-cost embarrassment: its cost score equals B's. The recommendation would be the same with shuffled labels and no incumbent. R2 withdrawals are not votes; only the discovery limitation, semantic counterexample and explicitly unmeasured bootstrap affect the judgment. Agreement is not evidence. These controls continue the R2 procedure (`03-Reports/JUDGE-r2-questions.md:3-13`; `01-Bus.md:172-211`).

## Options Considered

Each strongest case FOR precedes its rebuttal. Options retain their topic identities; D without its prerequisite is a subordinate tactic, not a newly scored version of D.

## Decision

**Choose Option A as the single primary strategy, and reject literal Ant Design parity as the product goal.** The Skeptic established the framing defect: Godx deliberately routes CheckableTag to Toggle, uses tokens for visual controls, and excludes chat shadows; matching upstream names and defaults would erase product decisions (`02-Research/SKEPTIC.md:47-54`; `docs/roadmap/antd-parity.md:9-21`; `docs/roadmap/ai-chat-components.md:46-56`). The goal is exhaustive capability accountability against a pinned Ant core/X reference, followed by accepted Godx behavior. A complete ledger is not delivered parity.

**Single deciding rationale:** A preserves comprehensive discovery without making reviewed repairs depend on a global checker whose prerequisite benefit has not been demonstrated. B loses the wider denominator; D has not justified that dependency; C is inadmissible. This is a comparative judgment, not a measured speedup (`02-Research/ADV-B.md:108-120`; `02-Research/ADV-D.md:123-135`).

The hierarchy and order are binding: **A discovery and admission → B's consumer evidence for priority and acceptance of each repair → a bounded D pilot on reviewed rows when repair/review capacity permits.** Broader auditing may continue read-only while admitted repairs proceed. D never blocks unrelated reviewed repairs. C's compulsory surface port and packaging exception are rejected; its availability concern becomes explicit delivery milestones, not an implementation strategy.

**Scope.** Inventory all 127 baseline local entries, reconcile additions, and independently enumerate Ant Design core and Ant Design X UI capabilities, including those with no local counterpart. Record dated upstream version/URL and local file-content identity; do not guess versions or silently expand to other ecosystem products. Track nested APIs, refs, defaults, modes and relevant international behavior. Use PRESENT, RENAMED, COVERED-ELSEWHERE, MISSING and WONT-PORT, with a separate unreviewed/pending/verified/failed acceptance state. Uninspected regions remain visibly incomplete. Deferred virtualization stays MISSING; rejecting an Ant prop route must not conceal a missing token-backed capability. Report inventory completeness, open gaps and accepted behavior separately (`02-Research/ADV-A.md:35-41`; `02-Research/ADV-D.md:31-43,70`).

Preserve the published admission scope: Tree, CountBadge, List, Masonry, ChatBubble, ChatBubbleList, ChatComposer and ChatSuggestion; these are approved candidates, not claims that all need new files or already work. Extend Badge for removal and reuse it in TagInput; retain Toggle for selection, Upload for attachments, CodeBlock for code and AppProvider for provider behavior. Ribbon, Prompts, Welcome, Actions and Sources remain compositions. ConversationList and ThoughtChain remain conditional on proving the unresolved gates against NavList/ListRow and Timeline/Collapsible. List follows the narrow virtualization/grouping contract; Card/CardContent, Separator and Pagination retain framing/paging. Preserve TreeList's export/API and Badge's name (`docs/roadmap/tree-components.md:43-55,117-132`; `docs/roadmap/badge-tag-chip-count.md:51-63,105-146`; `docs/roadmap/list-masonry.md:15-55,88-100`; `docs/roadmap/ai-chat-components.md:18-38,157-174`).

**Composition boundary and authority.** The component author supplies evidence for C1–C7; a designated framework reviewer independent of that change author records the admission verdict. The consumer owner supplies the task and acceptance case, not a gate waiver. Any failed or unresolved criterion routes to composition, or extension of the existing owner; visual gaps require documented tokens. Published ledgers guide work but their economic assertions are not measurements. The binding seven-pass rule overrides borderline examples; no agent may create an Ant-shaped duplicate or invent a second vocabulary (`docs/COMPOSITION-VS-COMPONENT.md:30-42,56-68,79-86`; `00-Topic.md:62-76`; `02-Research/SKEPTIC.md:53`).

**Priority.** P0 requires a cited broken real Platform task; P1 requires the documented unsafe hand-rolled workaround; P2 is optional benefit. No new P0 is established by this ruling. A broken exported mode cannot be marked accepted at any priority. Proven P0s preempt the queue; otherwise finish the reviewed Tree slice, then the owned chat slices, then revalidate and address the documented Tabs overflow and Steps responsive P1 findings. CountBadge/Badge–TagInput work advances on evidenced P1 demand; List/Tree virtualization advances on an observed data/interaction requirement, before its dependent consumers. Masonry and unsupported-demand additions remain explicit backlog, not emergencies. Within a tier, prerequisites precede dependents, then prefer smaller reversible repairs (`docs/roadmap/antd-parity.md:38-39,55-81`; `02-Research/ADV-A.md:49-53`). At each weekly review, every deferred MISSING row needs an owner, next review date and delivery trigger; a committed milestone cannot close with required rows missing or unverified (`02-Research/ADV-C.md:104-110`; `02-Research/ADV-D.md:41`).

## Rubric & Scoring

Formula: total = coverage + correctness/international integrity + fit + cost/load + reversibility; the maxima are exactly **25 + 25 + 20 + 15 + 15 = 100**. Proposed controls receive partial credit, never outcome certification. A and D receive equal correctness scores because neither demonstrates semantic rejection; wrong-but-present behavior earns no presence credit.

## Execution

The first three work items, in order, target **September 10–11**. They are implementation instructions for a subsequent authorized phase; this ruling only edits the three requested debate files and runs no builds, tests, servers or commits. Caps below are **[unsupported estimates]**, with no promise that all implementation will finish inside them.

1. **Checkpoint Tree/chat ownership and publish the first inventory slice.** The coordinator obtains dirty-file manifests and explicit stop/release acknowledgments from the existing Tree, ChatBubble/ChatBubbleList and ChatComposer/ChatSuggestion owners. Pin the upstream scope; reconcile Tree against TreeList/TreeSelect, and the four chat primitives against their specifications. Publish reviewed versus uninspected rows and an index covering the 127 baseline entries plus upstream-only candidates; do not claim the full capability audit is finished. Start with the real modules `src/components/data-display/tree.tsx`, `src/components/data-display/chat-bubble.tsx`, `src/components/data-entry/chat-composer.tsx` and `src/components/data-entry/chat-suggestion.tsx` (`00-Topic.md:48-51`; `docs/roadmap/ai-chat-components.md:44,79,102,139`; `02-Research/ADV-A.md:37,130`). **Cap: 3 person-hours** for ownership and inventory setup; unfinished discovery stays open.

2. **Challenge Topbar/TopbarItem guidance and Cascader acceptance independently.** A reviewer who did not author the change checks the actual catalog advice, then tests whether a deliberately wrong Button recommendation with reciprocal names preserved is refused. For `src/components/data-entry/cascader.tsx`, require parent/intermediate selection, depth-three hover through leaf selection, search and every other declared interactive mode to reach its terminal state, with console errors/warnings recorded. The challenge must reject search-only evidence. Record exact content identities and rejected/failed/untested outcomes; do not inject deliberate regressions into the shared working tree. Use separate fixtures in the later implementation phase. This implements the historical challenge, not a claim that either current component is broken (`scripts/check-catalog-contradictions.mjs:9-16,34-46`; `.claude/skills/godxjp-ui-component/SKILL.md:143-155`; `src/components/data-entry/cascader.tsx:457-467`). **Cap: 6 person-hours**: fixtures 2, independent review 2, integration/evidence 0.5, serialized validation 1.5. Missing access leaves acceptance UNTESTED.

3. **Complete one reviewed Tree acceptance slice before widening data-display work.** Reconcile the existing Tree implementation with selection, expansion, checks, async behavior, keyboard and RTL requirements; repair evidenced failures, retain TreeList compatibility, and verify TreeSelect still uses the shared traversal contract. Own `src/components/data-display/tree.tsx`, its affected tests/docs/tokens and the serialized `src/lib/tree.ts` / `src/components/data-entry/tree-utils.ts` compatibility handoff; integrate catalog, registry and export changes through the sole integrator. Exit requires relevant framework checks, zero axe violations and real Platform evidence against locally built package output; a source export or build alone cannot close it (`docs/roadmap/tree-components.md:88-103,117-142`; `CLAUDE.md:8-10`). **Cap: 6 person-hours**: reconciliation 1, repairs 2, independent review 1, integration 1, validation 1. Exceeding the cap leaves the slice open with a revised estimate; it never reduces the acceptance contract. Tree virtualization and drag/drop remain separate MISSING follow-ups (`docs/roadmap/tree-components.md:81-82`).

**Agent-assignment rule:** one active writer per component group and one named integrator for all shared files. Tree precedes ChatBubble in the data-display write queue; a queued writer may review read-only. After acquiring its distinct manifest, the data-entry owner may continue ChatComposer/ChatSuggestion, preserving Japanese/Vietnamese IME non-submission, without competing for Tree's shared utility files (`docs/roadmap/ai-chat-components.md:121-133`). Existing auditors remain read-only; add no writers just to increase concurrency. The integrator exclusively owns `mcp/src/data/components.ts`, `src/props/registry.ts`, group prop files/barrels, shared styles, `src/tokens/base.css`, locale JSON and any package/checker configuration. Authors hand over proposed shared hunks rather than editing those files (`docs/roadmap/ai-chat-components.md:182-195`; `02-Research/ADV-A.md:57-59,130`).

Every lease records owner, exact paths and content identity. A holder acknowledges release before the next acknowledges acquisition; **no acquire means no write**. Conflicts or overdue leases queue work: time expiry never authorizes takeover from an unacknowledged writer. Cross-group changes acquire both affected groups in one serialized handoff. Preserve unrelated dirty work; no broad reset, cleanup or staging. One machine-wide validation owner runs one targeted test process, at most two workers, and batches necessary static checks; full suites stay in CI, which runs asynchronously in the later implementation phase (`00-Topic.md:75-79`; `CLAUDE.md:10-13`; `02-Research/SKEPTIC.md:54,58`).

Before every integration/acceptance and at each working day's end, compare affected file contents, including uncommitted changes, to the last accepted snapshot. Changes to implementation, dependencies, props, examples, catalog, styles, tokens or locales invalidate dependent acceptance. Uncertain dependency scope invalidates the whole affected group; shared changes reach all dependent groups. Independent revalidation is required, even if disposition remains PRESENT. This is a mandatory manual procedure with residual bypass risk, not an implemented detector (`02-Research/ADV-A.md:123-127`).

The initial three-item allocation totals **15 person-hours**, not 15 elapsed hours; shared integration, review and validation must be scheduled serially where roles overlap. Record actual time separately. Defer lower-priority work if capacity is unavailable. Only after reviewed repairs have capacity, permit a **six-person-hour D pilot** on reviewed Tabs mappings: reject missing keys, removed targets and unresolved types; report behavioral uncertainty separately. Expand only on demonstrated detection and measured cost, and defer the pilot if it consumes required repair/review capacity (`02-Research/ADV-A.md:113`; `02-Research/ADV-D.md:131`).

## Consequences

Discovery becomes broader than current Platform demand without obligating speculative exports. Existing ownership and deliberate differences remain explicit: Tabs' local panel-unmount default, for example, is not automatically replaced merely to match Ant (`src/props/components/navigation.prop.ts:315-321`). The retained reference pattern is Ant core/X capability accounting; public APIs/tokens, en/vi/ja, IME safety, logical CSS and actual Platform acceptance remain the Godx contract. This ADR preserves those decisions; it certifies no rendered UI (`00-Topic.md:66-76`; `docs/roadmap/ai-chat-components.md:125-133,182-195`; `CLAUDE.md:6-10`).

Human review and shared integration become explicit scheduling costs. “Accounted for,” “implemented” and “accepted” can no longer be collapsed into one completion percentage. It remains possible to overlook a behavior or retain an expensive deferred backlog; the manual invalidation process reduces neither risk to zero (`02-Research/SKEPTIC.md:11-15,51-60`; `02-Research/ADV-A.md:124-130`).

## Dissent (mandatory)

- **B's spending objection survives its withdrawal:** discovery alone does not justify shipment, and Platform demand may be much narrower than the reference. Revisit B as primary if measured inventory/review costs exceed its demonstrated benefit and the user explicitly narrows the discovery promise; first-consumer status alone is insufficient evidence (`02-Research/ADV-B.md:55-57,110-120`).
- **D's persistence objection remains the strongest minority case:** manual checkpoints can leave stale acceptance. If one stale accepted row survives the independent Cascader-mode or names-intact guidance challenge, withdraw reliance on that control and withhold affected acceptance. Promote D only if it rejects the same escape and measured bootstrap, maintenance and integration costs fit the remaining delivery window; a structural green check alone is insufficient (`02-Research/SKEPTIC.md:73`; `02-Research/ADV-D.md:125-133`). A gains no immunity from its 2-point lead.
- **C's availability objection remains valid despite disqualification:** a MISSING row is unavailable capability. Revisit the delivery sequence when an observed Platform Tree dataset/task misses an agreed interaction budget and a committed virtualization milestone remains undelivered; compare strategies using the same device, dataset, deadline and complete costs. No such measurement exists here. Reconsider C itself only after an explicit policy change plus evidence of cross-consumer assembly savings and a credible migration budget (`02-Research/ADV-C.md:9-12,57-59,104-112`).
- **The Skeptic's unresolved risk is retained:** neither manual review, a green map nor agent agreement proves semantic correctness or enforceable scheduling. Approved List/Masonry C7 claims are not measured economics; a missing owner acknowledgment or unavailable reviewer stops the affected write/acceptance, not the clock on a fictional pass. Revisit admission economics on actual bundle/usage evidence and narrow accepted scope when review capacity fails (`02-Research/SKEPTIC.md:51-60,73-77`).

**Confidence accounting:** R2's 90/100 assessed the usefulness of cross-examination, not an option; this is an initial **68/100** confidence in the ruling, so there is no comparable numeric delta. The arguments determining this outcome are B's unclosed discovery boundary, D's names-intact semantic escape and unsupported coordinator budget, and A's admitted manual stale-acceptance interval. The latter limits confidence rather than disappearing because rivals withdrew (`03-Reports/JUDGE-r2-questions.md:9`; `01-Bus.md:172-211`).

Overall confidence: 68%.
